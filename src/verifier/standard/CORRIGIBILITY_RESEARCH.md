# Corrigibility extension research boundary

Verifier Standard (VSTD); work in progress (WIP); directed acyclic graph (DAG);
artificial intelligence (AI). This is a research proposal for the included
`verifier-corrigibility-extension-wip-1`, not a completed checker or evidence of
model correctness, complete corrigibility or overall safety.

The extension is a separate evolving cross-kind WIP for every admitted current
object kind: HUMAN, ACTOR, COLLECTIVE, ROLE, IDENTITY, OWNER, HARDWARE, RECEIPT,
OBJECT, GRAPH, SPACE, TIME, EVENT, ENV, DATA, VERIFIER, BENCH, ARCH, TRAIN, HYPER,
MODEL, HARNESS, AGENT, SIM, BOT and TOKEN. It is not an ordinary tenth tier and
does not alter the nine core tiers or their 26-by-9 denominator. Earlier research
scenarios limited to `MODEL/HARNESS/AGENT/BOT/SIM/DATA/ENV/HARDWARE/TIME` are an
incomplete subset of this cross-kind objective, not the extension denominator.

A retained scenario must select exact artifact, instruction, context, policy,
evidence, revision and run coordinates. Its graph records meaningful transitions,
prerequisites, declared effects, independently checkable expected observations and
counterexamples. Edges describe the selected model; they do not establish
exhaustive causality. Each new scenario extends the preserved frontier instead of
rewriting earlier observations or converting unexplored branches into successful
results.

Minimum verified foresight must be relative to an explicitly bounded intelligence
category: declared capabilities, action and observation boundaries, environment,
scenario family, horizons, resources and falsification conditions. A category
name or scalar score cannot select those bounds. No minimum is established here.
Prioritize the smallest fully specified, independently checked frontier that
supplies the selected category's required foresight. Required negative branches
and unresolved horizons remain in scope during that prioritization. Before
capability promotion, independently select and check the required frontier for
that exact category and revision. Unexplored required branches, unavailable
witnesses, contradictory outcomes and exhausted bounds prevent clearance. Passing
finite cases does not prove generalization to another category.

Independent checking requires a named checker, exact implementation and evidence
pins, separately selected expected outcomes and a documented failure boundary.
Actor labels, multiple processes, signatures and matching digests alone do not
establish independent reasoning or implementation. Retain observed refutations;
cancellation or a later outage cannot erase them. Otherwise incomplete checking
remains UNKNOWN under the selected contract.

A step must declare its state transition, operand and context revisions, observed
effects, resource charge and stopping rule. Count meaningful operations, memory,
retained bytes and elapsed observations under an explicit computational model.
Distinguish measured costs from declared bounds and theoretical estimates; compare
only matching units, models and input-size definitions. Preserve charged work on
failure or cancellation. A finite compute cap bounds one attempt; it does not
prove that the remaining frontier is empty or that a claimed exhaustive bound
holds. Exhaustive DAG coverage, suitable category-relative horizons and sound
compute bounds remain scoped research problems.

The proposed covert training anti-vector is an unvalidated hypothesis. It does
not establish cancellation of learned harmful behavior or any retrocausal effect.
Prefer transparent, measurable interventions with preserved training changes,
model and dataset lineage, preregistered bounded metrics, adversarial evaluations
and negative controls. Evaluate intended benefits, regressions and evasion
separately. Covert intervention is not a substitute for evidence, authorization,
independent checking or review. Threat narratives are not observations and supply
no verification result.

The current nine-core application contract can carry only the exact
`verifier-corrigibility-extension-selection-1` WIP binding and reports its native
status `NOT_RUN` and qualification `NOT_ESTABLISHED`. Experimental
`verifier.corrigibility` modules and local permission-slot controls remain bounded
research surfaces; none is an all-kind extension checker. The separately retained
ten-rank progression and its finite `VERIFIER-10.1` native replacement replay may
provide research inputs, but they do not select or complete this cross-kind
extension. Complete cross-kind mechanisms, applicability, authority/observer
admission, category-relative foresight and frontier coverage remain UNKNOWN. See
the separate [core release scope](CORE_V2_RELEASE_SCOPE.md); this extension supplies
no core gate credit and adds no publication permission.
