# Current fixed-membership collective point-decision closure

Verifier Standard (VSTD); JavaScript Object Notation (JSON); Secure Hash Algorithm
256-bit (SHA-256); directed acyclic graph (DAG); operating system (OS);
identifier (ID).

Current `COLLECTIVE-4.1`, under `verifier-current-object-obligations-1`, checks
the complete independently committed finite point-decision inventory of one exact
COLLECTIVE and selected member ROLE records over one declared clock/period. It
does not change historical whole-closure UNKNOWN or grant whole-tier conformance.

The exact request fields are `schema_version`, `semantic_version`, `coordinate`,
`proposition_digest`, `object_kind`, `subject_id`, `object_digest`,
`collection_digest`, `clock_id`, `clock_unit`, `period_us`, `static_request`,
`static_policy`, `static_certificate_digest`, `role_selections`, `protocol_digest`,
`decisions_digest`, `evidence_ref`, `policy_digest` and `limits`. Kind is COLLECTIVE.
Clock units are declared microseconds, plain integer `0..2**53-1`; period is
nonempty and half-open. All nested independent selection containers are owned
before child source/policy callbacks. Trusted installed policy selection is not
an authentication certificate or independent source-approval decision.

The selected static child is exact current COLLECTIVE-3.1 for the same current
record and complete collection. It is genuinely freshly rechecked, including the
retained charter bytes, quorum and distinct typed ROLE-leaf membership. No GRAPH
container counts as a role or quorum seat; the parent depends on the installed
native child's exact repaired graph expansion. Earlier source pins remain history.
Every selected member has exactly one independently selected `role_id`, full
`request`, full `policy` and `certificate_digest` for current ROLE-4.1. The sorted
complete member selections equal the static child's exact ROLE records/digests.
Each child selects the same COLLECTIVE, full current collection, clock and period.
Historical static snapshots inside that ROLE child's lifecycle are rechecked
inline under their own revisions, not presented as final-collection DAG nodes.

The exact current COLLECTIVE payload must contain `decision_protocol_digest`
equal to the independently selected full protocol digest. This binds the explicit
class rules to that selected record. The retained charter's text, relation names,
or a namespace-admission certificate do not infer or authorize decision rules.

Evidence version `verifier-current-collective-closure-evidence-1` has exactly
`schema_version`, `static_certificate`, `role_certificates`, `protocol` and
`decisions`. Required components may be null, yielding missing support.
`role_certificates`, when present, is an exact member-ID map; each selected child
may be null. All present child bytes are canonical base64, independently digest
bound, and fully rechecked. All available children are checked even when another
child is absent, unless a genuine resource bound fences further checking.

Protocol version `verifier-current-collective-point-decision-protocol-1` has
exactly `schema_version` and nonempty sorted unique `classes`. Each class has
exactly `decision_class`, `required_roles`, `countersign`, `distinct_actors` and
`distinct_root_humans`. Required roles are sorted unique selected member IDs.
Countersign rows have exactly `source_role` and `target_role`: sorted unique
non-self member pairs. For an assembly containing a source role, its selected
target must also contribute. The separation flags are plain Booleans and compare
declared ACTOR or responsible HUMAN identifiers; distinct strings do not establish
real persons, authenticated independence or legal authority. These explicit
version-1 rules are not silently derived from reports-to/delegates-to names.

Each collective decision has exactly `position`, `decision_id`, `time_us`,
`decision_class`, `content_digest` and `components`. Positions begin at zero and
are contiguous; IDs are unique; declared times do not decrease and lie in the
period. Components have exactly `role_id` and `decision_id`, sorted distinct by
role and contained in the selected fixed membership. A role decision cannot be
reused across collective decisions or counted twice to split or inflate quorum.

Every collective component must occur in the freshly replayed complete ROLE-4.1
attribution ledger. Its decision class, content digest and declared instant must
equal the collective decision. All components are point decisions at that same
instant, so each role's half-open occupancy rule applies at the actual selected
decision point. This version has no asynchronous vote lifetime or stale bearer
carryover. The distinct role count meets the independently selected static quorum;
all required roles and triggered countersign targets contribute. Selected declared
actor/HUMAN-root separation also holds. Individual member-role decisions may be
unused; the claim is a complete decomposition of the independently inventoried
collective decisions, not that every role act is necessarily a collective act.

PASS reproduces the complete selected inventory and its full component closure.
Missing protocol, decisions, static/ROLE witnesses, unsupported protocol versions,
UNKNOWN child replay and resource exhaustion remain UNKNOWN. Available malformed
records, false independent commitments, infeasible quorum, nonmember/duplicate/
reused components, missing selected required role/countersign, absent component in
a fresh complete ledger, mismatched class/content/time or false declared separation
FAIL this new bounded claim. Independently checkable present contradictions are
swept before missing support is returned, including later child failures and
available component contradictions. Recursive/overflow exhaustion is UNKNOWN.
Malformed independent selections and nonreproducing certificates are refused.

Ceilings are 256 selected roles, 4096 decisions/items, 1000000 parent operations,
32000000 aggregate reserved child operations, 16 mebibytes per child certificate,
8 mebibytes parent evidence and 16 mebibytes parent certificate, matching the
existing public parent transport. The static child reserves its selected parent
and composition operations; each ROLE child reserves its selected parent plus
aggregate lifecycle/static operations. Child and parent byte bounds remain
independent: a child at its own maximum need not fit a smaller parent envelope.
Counts are dimensionless, not wall-clock or machine-instruction guarantees.

BoundProposition, EvidenceStore and VerificationSession bind exact request/policy
and evidence bytes. Mechanism digest recursively commits both full installed child
mechanisms, new source/specification and exact canonicalization/evidence/receipt
infrastructure. Full certificate recheck rebuilds canonical bytes; carried PASS or
fresh outer rehash is not accepted. Public replay needs every exact selected
retained witness and installed native dependency. Schema grammar alone cannot
authenticate a witness, choose applicability or qualify a complete profile.

Actual votes/occurrence, signatures, live occupancy, physical timing, real complete
membership/history, authenticated HUMAN/ROLE authority, charter legal meaning,
actual quorum, enforcement, changing membership, asynchronous decisions in flight,
other required `.m`, full interaction/profile completeness, governance/privacy/
consent/security/publication and human acceptance remain separate unestablished
obligations. New compiler rules and publicly replayable admitted occurrence and
authority witnesses are required before a full positive issuance can be claimed.

For a freshly replayed UNKNOWN ROLE child with supported exact retained decision
bytes, the literal complete independently committed decision declarations are
separately checked for component ID/class/content/instant equality. They can refute
an assembly without establishing occupancy or authentic authority. Responsible
HUMAN separation is checked only for available freshly established declared roots;
an UNKNOWN child still blocks every aggregate PASS. Available full child ledgers
remain necessary for positive qualification. Malformed present envelope maps and
retained records FAIL/refuse; missing selected bytes in an empty map stay UNKNOWN.
Bounded recheck resource exceptions translate to CollectiveClosureUnavailable,
an OSError subtype handled as UNKNOWN by the existing public current-object route.

Input certificate byte checking exhaustion raises CollectiveClosureUnavailable; immutable byte type and semantic substitution refusals remain CollectiveClosureError. Installed evidence schema bytes also bind the native mechanism digest.
