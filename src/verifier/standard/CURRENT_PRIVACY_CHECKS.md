## Registered proposition

Semantic version `verifier-current-object-obligations-1` registers `<kind>-6.7`
for each of the 26 retained native `ObjectKind` values through
`verifier.domains.contracts.privacy_checks`. This additional current objective
does not reinterpret legacy disclosure coordinates 6.1 through 6.6 or claim
coverage of the 12 added consequential roles in the 38-kind extensible namespace.

The exact selected namespace record must occur in its bound, structurally valid
collection. Its complete canonical record must remain unchanged after the native
disclosure evaluator applies independently selected field enumeration, declarative
bounds and observer facts. Its selected co-disclosures must trigger no bundled or
additional typed refusal rule. Each of the four whole fields, `object_id`, `kind`,
`payload` and `operands`, requires enumeration and a permitting bound. A bound for
`payload` covers that complete value; nested-path permissions are unsupported.

This is a full object-record admission predicate, not a certificate-validity or
certificate-emission mechanism. Payloads may contain unverified claims or private
data. Their presence in a permitted field does not establish their truth, permission
to publish them, or permission to disclose this assessment's witnesses and sidecars.

## Independent selection and native evaluation

The request binds the semantic version, exact numbered proposition, represented
kind, subject, record digest, collection digest, evidence bytes, policy, installed
mechanism, context timestamp label and resource bounds. The policy separately
selects the observer's declared actor, role, purpose and credentials; disclosed
and withheld fields; declarative field bounds; co-emitted subject identifiers;
and additional refusal rules. Evidence cannot select its own acceptance conditions.

The timestamp is an independently selected nonempty text label, not a trusted
clock or temporal permission. Neither the label nor the computation establishes
that an emission occurred. Consent, governance and freshness need separate checks.

Observer and role membership retains the native union rule. Conditions within a
bound are conjunctive, including repeated keys; separate bounds are alternatives.
Unsupported conditions do not admit a field. Custom containers and callback
predicates are refused before callbacks. Policy records are bounded and copied
before evaluation so mutation of the original selection cannot change the check.

The native namespace mechanism checks exact records, resolved operands and acyclic
composition. Co-emitted identifiers must select other actual records in the same
collection. Native composition screening uses those records' admitted kinds rather
than untrusted payload labels. Bundled refusal pairs always apply; additional
rules can add restrictions. Passing the listed screen is not general privacy of
correlations. Undeclared real-world disclosures are outside the selected model.

The bundled `EmissionEvaluator` checks whole-field bounds and preserves verdict
labels. This contract additionally requires the complete selected record to be
byte-identical after evaluation. A missing bound, denied field, withheld field,
incomplete enumeration or typed refusal pair refutes the selected full-record
proposition. Missing evidence and exhausted selected bounds remain UNKNOWN.

The policy binds core and native privacy source bytes plus this specification.
Rechecking rebuilds the entire canonical certificate under the external request
and policy. A caller cannot repair a substituted result by recomputing its hash.
This records local deterministic replay, not loaded-code authenticity.

## Bounds and result boundaries

Default ceilings are 256 namespace objects; 100,000 disclosure work units;
100,000 separate namespace composition operations; 4 MiB evidence, payload and
reference bounds; and 8 MiB certificate bytes. Caller-selected lower limits bind.
Disclosure work charges structural visits through retained evidence and policy,
typed pair/rule comparisons and conservative whole-field predicate allowances.
These are abstract work units, not machine instructions, elapsed time or memory
measurements. Native repeated copying has bounded input size but no independent
instruction counter. Retained disclosure depth is at most 64.

Policy capture permits at most 50,000 traversed values, depth 64, 1 MiB aggregate
text, containers of 4,096 entries and integers of 4,096 bits. There are at most
256 field bounds, 64 conditions per bound, 64 entries per selection string list
and 64 additional rules. Policy representation violations refuse selection.

The local checking result reports no emitted record and performs no transmission.
Its portable certificate retains the complete evidence collection; that certificate
is itself disclosure-sensitive and needs separate admission before any transfer.
The command's result contains bounded status and digests, not the raw namespace
records. Digests are unsalted bindings, not hiding commitments or public proofs.

Observer authentication, general composition safety, underlying payload/certificate
validity, actual transmission, consent, governance, authority, complete privacy-tier
conformance and polished-artifact issuance remain NOT_ESTABLISHED. A successful
disclosure check cannot strengthen any computational verdict inside the record.
