# Tier-1 facet-reason ledger

**Status:** installed source-bound reason gate version 3. The ledger keeps 130
five-facet choices separate from 18 domain-route necessity choices. Their exact
reasoned union covers the current 52 structural memberships, 45 registered
Tier-1 routes, and 26 Tier-1 slots. No result here is a route certificate outcome,
polished status, or release, publication, deployment, or external authority.

Verifier's installed Tier-1 semantic denominator has five choices for each of the
26 admitted namespace object kinds:

1. `identifier`
2. `admitted_kind`
3. `canonical_payload`
4. `named_ordered_operand_map`
5. `declared_typed_composition`

That is 130 independently retained choices. A slot marked `REQUIRED` does not
explain any one of these choices. The 26 existing slot rationales are retained as
planning context with
`rationale_status=PLANNING_CONTEXT_NOT_NUMBERED_REQUIREMENTS`; they qualify for
zero reason-ledger entries.

## Installed ledger

`installed_facet_reason_ledger()` returns
`verifier-self-polish-facet-reason-ledger-3`. Each facet choice binds exactly one
`object_kind`, `tier=1`, and facet. Its decision vocabulary is `NECESSARY`,
`NOT_NECESSARY`, or `UNKNOWN`. The installed version explicitly chooses
`NECESSARY` for every current choice and supplies:

- nonblank source-authored reason text and its digest;
- a counterfactual consequence of omitting the facet;
- source-authored provenance and provenance digest;
- the exact installed typed-registration basis and basis digest;
- the digest of all mechanism and denominator source bindings;
- mapped structural identities, only directly derived generic or typed Tier-1
  route identities with an explicit route-mapping rationale, and its Tier-1 slot
  identity; and
- an exact choice digest.

Primitive object kinds do not receive an applicability waiver for composition.
Their typed-composition and operand-map reasons identify exact emptiness as a
positive constraint: the installed checker must establish that the role set is
empty. Omission cannot silently convert this obligation to not applicable.

For every nonprimitive kind, the basis and the admitted-kind, operand-map, and
typed-composition reasons state the actual installed contract: exact or dynamic
role names, minimum and maximum role/target counts, admitted target kinds, the
4,096-reference total bound, graph expansion, reference-resolution and acyclicity
invariants, plus ACTOR, GRAPH, or HYPER special conditions where applicable. The
contract is visible structured data and prose, not an opaque digest or the phrase
"installed role contract."

The mechanism binds current bytes for the reason producer, this specification,
namespace kinds and composition plus `NAMESPACE_OBJECTS.md`; generic OBJECT facets
plus `CURRENT_OBJECT_FACETS.md`; typed facets plus `CURRENT_TYPED_FACETS.md`; the
executable facet mapping plus `FACET_OBLIGATION_DERIVATION.md`; application
structural rules; the installed route inventory mechanism; and the 234-slot
inventory source. Each file is used directly. Replaying an old ledger after any
bound source changes therefore fails closed.

The source coordinates are deliberately not interchangeable. `identifier` and
`canonical_payload` ground in generic `OBJECT-1.1`. `declared_typed_composition`
grounds in the kind's typed coordinate (`OBJECT-1.2` for OBJECT and the kind's
`1.1` otherwise). `admitted_kind` and `named_ordered_operand_map` bind both source
authorities because generic representation commits their exact fields while the
typed registration gives those values their composition constraint. The actual
structural derivation remains narrower: the four generic facets map only to the
generic predicate, and declared typed composition maps only to the typed predicate.

The ledger also carries exactly 18 `domain_route_reasons`. They are not sixth
facet choices and do not inflate the 130-choice denominator. Each row binds one
registered Tier-1 route identity—including object kind, coordinate, proposition,
scope, certificate schema, and checker mechanism—to its current route-catalog
registration, checker source bytes, a distinct domain category, an explicit
Tier-1 necessity rationale, and a concrete counterfactual. `DATA-1.2` additionally
binds the existing `selected_retained_data_inventory` authored property from
`facet_coverage.py`. A route registration establishes mechanism, not necessity;
the separate authored reason explains why a structurally valid namespace object
could otherwise escape that exact domain semantic.

The 18 categories are the harness binding for AGENT; problem inventory and oracle
execution for BENCH; child binding for BOT; retained inventory and record schema
for DATA; software closure, executable coordinates, required configuration
surface, and retained configuration for ENV; containment topology and device
inventory for HARDWARE; observation surface for HARNESS; dense tensor shape and
artifact dependency binding for MODEL; and configuration, checkpoint inventory,
and data-shard/batch binding for TRAIN.

## Assessment semantics

`assess_facet_reason_choices(choices, domain_route_reasons=None)` compares both
candidate registries with the exact installed source-authored rows. Omitting the
second argument means that no domain-route reasons were supplied; it never
silently selects the installed rows and therefore remains `UNKNOWN` at 27/45
routes and 17/26 slots even when all 130 facet reasons reproduce. A missing row or
otherwise-valid missing reason in either registry is `UNKNOWN` and blocks
completeness. Missing text does not bypass validation: choice, source binding,
basis, provenance, route identity or mappings, and recomputed row digest must all
reproduce first. A malformed row, duplicate identity, cross-kind or cross-route
identity, substituted text/basis/provenance, contradictory choice, or digest
mismatch is `FAIL`. No omission infers `NOT_NECESSARY` or not applicable.

`replay_facet_reason_ledger(ledger)` additionally validates the complete ledger,
its source bindings, projections, and ledger digest. The no-argument
`installed_facet_reason_assessment()` replays the current installed ledger and
does not require a configured signing key or external authority.

The assessment re-derives three projections from qualifying reasons:

- 52 installed structural memberships from the five structural facets;
- all 45 registered Tier-1 replay routes from the union of 27 routes directly
  derived from the five facets and 18 separately reasoned domain routes; and
- all 26 Tier-1 object-kind slots for which every registered route is reasoned.

The 18 routes are retained exactly in `unmatched_route_projection` because they
remain unmatched by the five structural facets; that name does not mean they lack
reasons in v3. Their separate rows cover AGENT, BENCH, BOT, DATA, ENV, HARDWARE,
HARNESS, MODEL, and TRAIN. A generic/typed facet may carry bytes used by such a
route, but carrier status is not a reason for that domain predicate. If the second
registry is omitted, those 18 routes and 9 affected slots remain `UNKNOWN`; when
all exact domain rows reproduce, the union closes 45/45 routes and 26/26 slots.

Every projection includes its expected and reason-derived digest. Projection
counts do not replace the 130 per-choice reasons. Conversely, 130 reason rows do
not qualify a projection unless their exact mapped identities reproduce the
current installed denominator.

`facet_choice_reason_status=PASS` means the 130 five-facet reasons reproduce, and
`domain_route_reason_status=PASS` means the separate 18 route reasons reproduce.
Aggregate `PASS` additionally requires their projection union to reason every
registered route and therefore every slot. Native replay, semantic sufficiency
beyond these installed choices, full repository/package closure, independent
authority, polished status, and release authorization remain separate gates.
