# Current finite ROLE point-decision closure

Verifier Standard (VSTD); JavaScript Object Notation (JSON); Secure Hash Algorithm
256-bit (SHA-256); directed acyclic graph (DAG); operating system (OS).

Current `ROLE-4.1`, under `verifier-current-object-obligations-1`, checks one
complete externally selected finite declared point-decision and occupancy history.
It does not reinterpret historical ROLE-4 rows or their UNKNOWN-only whole-profile
closure result. Registration, PASS and selected inventory completeness do not
establish the whole ROLE closure tier or any object's complete applicable profile.

The native request has exactly `schema_version`, `semantic_version`, `coordinate`,
`proposition_digest`, `object_kind`, `subject_id`, `object_digest`,
`collection_digest`, `collective_id`, `clock_id`, `clock_unit`, `period_us`,
`endpoint_times_us`, `lifecycle_request`, `lifecycle_policy`,
`lifecycle_certificate_digest`, `events_digest`, `decisions_digest`, `evidence_ref`,
`policy_digest` and `limits`. Subject kind is ROLE. All commitments and the complete
request/policy are selected independently of a carried certificate. The child is
exact current ROLE-2.1, its final ROLE record and collection equal the parent, and
its full request, policy and certificate are independently bound. Its complete
retained ROLE-3.1 snapshots are genuinely replayed inline under their own exact
selected record/collection revisions. A carried or newly rehashed PASS is not a
prerequisite. Earlier snapshots are not presented as final-collection DAG nodes.

The new version-1 time interpretation is explicit: one selected declared clock,
microsecond units, bounded plain integer instants `0..2**53-1`, and a nonempty
half-open period `[start_us,end_us)`. This is not a physical-clock claim. There is
one independently selected time per child endpoint, the first equals the start,
all times strictly increase and remain before the end. Occupancy persists from
each endpoint until the next, and the final endpoint until the selected end.

Evidence version `verifier-current-role-closure-evidence-1` has exactly
`schema_version`, `lifecycle_certificate`, `events` and `decisions`. A required
component may be null, yielding missing support. The child certificate is canonical
base64. The complete event/decision arrays must reproduce their independent digest
commitments. Each event has exactly `position`, `time_us`, `action`, `taking` and
`leaving`; position begins at one and equals its destination endpoint. Time equals
that endpoint's selected time. Taking/leaving are sorted unique ACTOR identifiers:
TAKE has nonempty taking only; LEAVE nonempty leaving only; HANDOVER exactly one
distinct leaving and taking actor atomically; CHECKPOINT neither. The exact deltas
must match the freshly replayed child endpoints. No missing intermediate history
is inferred from endpoint equality. Completeness means the selected committed
inventory; complete real event capture remains NOT_ESTABLISHED.

The endpoints form disjoint time cells carrying occupancy SETS. Every cell must
have at least one occupant. Individual bearer tenures may overlap when their
selected static bound permits it. There is no invented universal single-bearer
rule. Each decision has exactly `position`, `decision_id`, `time_us`, `actor_id`,
`identity_id`, `scope`, `decision_class` and `content_digest`. Positions begin at
zero and are contiguous; identifiers are unique; times do not decrease and lie in
the selected period. Half-open containment assigns a decision at a handover to the
incoming state. The exact selected endpoint occupancy matrix must bind that
IDENTITY, ACTOR, ROLE and scope; the ROLE's admitted scopes include the scope; its
literal permission list contains the decision-class token. Token equality is the
explicit version-1 interpretation, not permission issuance or legal authority.
The declared responsible HUMAN is retained separately and is not authenticated.

PASS reproduces the full bounded history and all these attributions. Absent
components, unavailable/UNKNOWN child replay, unsupported evidence versions,
byte/operation/relation limits and missing retained witness inputs remain UNKNOWN.
Malformed present records, changed independent bindings, wrong event order/deltas,
an empty occupancy cell, duplicate or out-of-period decisions and false
actor/identity/scope/permission attribution FAIL this new bounded claim. Available
independent contradictions are checked before missing support is reported; resource
exhaustion can fence further checking without manufacturing an unobserved verdict.
Malformed independent selections and nonreproducing certificates are refused.

Default ceilings are 4096 decisions/items, 1000000 parent operations, 100000 shared
identity relation references, 9700000 reserved child operations, 16 mebibytes per
child certificate, 8 mebibytes of parent evidence and 16 mebibytes per parent
certificate. The child reserves its own selected parent plus aggregate child
operation ceilings and independently enforces every native bound. The existing
ROLE lifecycle permits at most 16 endpoints. Counts are dimensionless. Nesting is
preflighted before recursive parsing. These ceilings are not machine instructions,
wall-clock guarantees or an OS sandbox. Smaller positive limits are binding.

The mechanism digest commits this source/specification, canonicalization,
evidence/receipt/retained-selection infrastructure, identity relation expansion and
the complete installed lifecycle/static mechanism digest. Source changes invalidate
old policy selection. BoundProposition, EvidenceStore and VerificationSession bind
exact evidence references and produce a retained result. Recheck reconstructs the
entire canonical certificate from independent request/policy and exact retained
bytes. Public replay needs all selected bytes and installed native dependencies.

Actual occurrence, live occupancy, physical timing, complete real-world history,
human authenticity, authenticated authority, actor independence, legal permission,
delegation, personal acts and asynchronous decisions across handover remain
unestablished. Public occurrence/authority witnesses require separate admitted
protocols and replay, not a trust-root label, source signature or namespace-admission
certificate. Privacy, consent, governance, security, every other required `.m`,
full-profile completeness, human acceptance and positive issuance remain separate.

The parent ceilings match the existing public current-object and canonical
emission transport maximum of 16 mebibytes. A separately selected child byte bound
is a checking ceiling, not a promise that every child at that ceiling fits the
parent evidence envelope. Parent evidence/retention exhaustion stays UNKNOWN or
refuses a certificate beyond the external policy; it cannot issue a larger public
certificate through a smaller transport. No transport extension is proposed.

Independent period and endpoint arrays, child endpoint selections/limits and child
trust-root labels are captured into owned plain values before child source/policy
callbacks. Caller mutation cannot alter a previously captured native selection.

Parent limits are captured before child policy/source callbacks as well. Resource
Unavailable, RecursionError and OverflowError in carried-certificate preflight
are translated to the explicit OSError subtype RoleClosureUnavailable, which the
existing public current-object route reports UNKNOWN; recheck
does not relabel them as a malformed-certificate semantic refutation.

Every public request/build/recheck entrypoint owns all incoming plain nested
selection containers and parent policy/root labels before the first installed
source callback. Policy construction owns supplied root labels before hashing.
Capture admits only plain dict/list/string/integer/Boolean/null values, at most
200000 visited selection values and depth96; foreign callbacks and floats are
refused. This independent-input bound is separate from native evidence budgets.

When an exact freshly replayed lifecycle remains UNKNOWN, supported available
endpoint ROLE-3.1 witnesses are independently freshly replayed under their exact
selected request/policy/certificate commitments. Local static attribution and
adjacent available occupancy deltas can still FAIL; unavailable endpoints block
only their predicates. The complete lifecycle prerequisite remains UNKNOWN, so
these local checks never fabricate aggregate PASS. Additional static replay
reserves each selected parent's composition/relation/domain ceilings beyond the
original full lifecycle reserve, under the 9700000 aggregate parent ceiling.
Decoded child certificates and retained evidence are depth-preflighted before
inherited native readers so unavailable depth work is not relabelled FAIL by an
inherited ValueError wrapper. Exact child-result coordinate/kind/scope/version and
NOT_ESTABLISHED authority/profile boundary are checked before using native data.

Retained child extraction first validates exact plain certificate/payload/evidence
record shapes and canonical independent bindings. Malformed present maps/records
FAIL or are refused; an absent selected payload in a genuine empty map is UNKNOWN.

Input certificate byte checking exhaustion raises RoleClosureUnavailable; immutable byte type and semantic substitution refusals remain RoleClosureError. The installed evidence schema bytes also bind the native mechanism digest.
